# AVIATION HRM MASTER CHEAT SHEET (UNITS 1 - 3)

## UNIT 1: INTRODUCTION TO HRM
*   **Definition:** The strategic approach to managing an organization's most valued assets—the people working there.
*   **Key Functions:**
    *   *Managerial:* Planning, Organizing, Directing, Controlling.
    *   *Operative:* Recruitment, Training, Compensation, Maintenance, Integration.
*   **SHRM (Strategic HRM):** Aligning HR practices with the overall business strategy (e.g., hiring multilingual cabin crew for international expansion).
*   **Job Analysis:** 
    *   *Job Description:* The "What" (Duties, responsibilities, reporting structure).
    *   *Job Specification:* The "Who" (Qualifications, skills, physical requirements).
*   **HRIS (Human Resource Information System):** Digital systems used to track employee data, payroll, and performance (e.g., Workday, SAP).

## UNIT 2: RECRUITMENT & SELECTION
*   **Recruitment (Positive Process):** Attracting a large pool of qualified candidates.
    *   *Internal:* Promotions, transfers (Boosts morale, cheaper).
    *   *External:* Campus drives, portals, agencies (Brings fresh ideas).
*   **Selection (Negative Process):** Filtering out unsuitable candidates to find the best fit.
*   **Selection Process Steps:** Screening -> Written Tests -> Interviews -> Background Verification -> Medical Exam -> Final Offer.
*   **Types of Tests:** 
    *   *Aptitude:* Measures potential to learn new skills.
    *   *Psychometric:* Evaluates personality traits and psychological fit (Crucial for pilots/cabin crew).
*   **Induction/Orientation:** Welcoming and integrating new hires into the company culture.

## UNIT 3: TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT
*   **Training:** Short-term, focused on improving specific current job skills (e.g., Emergency Evacuation Drill).
*   **Development:** Long-term, focused on overall growth and preparing for future roles (e.g., Leadership Development Program).
*   **Training Methods:**
    *   *On-the-Job (OJT):* Job rotation, mentoring, coaching (Learning while doing).
    *   *Off-the-Job:* Simulators, classroom lectures, role-playing (Safe environment for high-risk jobs like aviation).
*   **Performance Appraisal:** Evaluating an employee's current and past performance relative to their performance standards.
    *   *360-Degree Feedback:* Feedback from peers, managers, subordinates, and customers.
*   **Evaluating Effectiveness (Kirkpatrick Model):** Reaction -> Learning -> Behavior -> Results.

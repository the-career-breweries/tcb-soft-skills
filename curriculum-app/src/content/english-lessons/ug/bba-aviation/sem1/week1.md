# Fundamentals of Grammar

---

# Parts of Speech
Every word in a sentence serves a specific purpose.

*   **Noun:** A person, place, thing, or idea (e.g., *Pilot, Airport, Freedom*).
*   **Verb:** An action or state of being (e.g., *Fly, Is, Communicate*).
*   **Adjective:** Describes a noun (e.g., *Turbulent, Fast, Efficient*).

---

# Activity: Sentence Structure
Identify the Subject, Verb, and Object in this sentence:

> "The captain announced the descent."

*   **Subject:** The captain
*   **Verb:** announced
*   **Object:** the descent

---

# Tenses Overview
*   **Past:** Actions completed before now (e.g., *The flight landed.*)
*   **Present:** Actions happening now or habitually (e.g., *The flight lands at 5 PM.*)
*   **Future:** Actions that will happen (e.g., *The flight will land.*)

# The 8 Parts of Speech (Part 1)
Every word in a sentence serves a specific structural purpose. 

*   **Noun:** Names a person, place, thing, or concept. They are the "who" or "what" of a sentence.
    *(e.g., The **pilot** landed the **aircraft** at the **airport**.)*
*   **Pronoun:** Replaces a noun to avoid repetition.
    *(e.g., **She** briefed the cabin crew before **they** took off.)*
*   **Verb:** Shows an action, occurrence, or state of being. The engine of the sentence.
    *(e.g., The plane **cruises** at 35,000 feet. The weather **is** clear.)*

---

# The 8 Parts of Speech (Part 2)
Modifiers add detail, color, and precision to our nouns and verbs.

*   **Adjective:** Modifies or describes a noun or pronoun. Tells us "which one", "what kind", or "how many".
    *(e.g., The **turbulent** flight required **immediate** action from the **two** captains.)*
*   **Adverb:** Modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb. Often ends in -ly. Tells us "how", "when", or "where".
    *(e.g., The ATC spoke **calmly** and **very clearly** during the emergency.)*

---

# The 8 Parts of Speech (Part 3)
Connectors and emotional markers bring sentences together.

*   **Preposition:** Shows the relationship (often in time or space) between a noun and another word.
    *(e.g., The passengers walked **through** the terminal and boarded **before** noon.)*
*   **Conjunction:** Connects words, phrases, or clauses (FANBOYS: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So).
    *(e.g., We requested clearance, **but** the runway was occupied, **so** we held our position.)*
*   **Interjection:** A word expressing sudden emotion or surprise. Usually stands alone.
    *(e.g., **Brace!** The impact is imminent. **Oh**, the flight is delayed again?)*

# Tenses Overview
*   **Past:** Actions completed before now (e.g., *The flight landed.*)
*   **Present:** Actions happening now or habitually (e.g., *The flight lands at 5 PM.*)
*   **Future:** Actions that will happen (e.g., *The flight will land.*)

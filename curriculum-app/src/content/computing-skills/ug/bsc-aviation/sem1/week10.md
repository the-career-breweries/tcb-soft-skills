* KEY CONCEPT

* Sorting & Filtering Data Explained

* SORT
* Rearranges every row in a table into a new order — for example, largest to smallest delay, or alphabetically by route. Every row stays in the table; only the order changes.
* Like reordering a deck of cards from highest to lowest.

* FILTER
* Temporarily hides rows that do not match a chosen condition, so only the rows of interest are visible. Hidden rows are not deleted — clearing the filter brings them back.
* Like looking through a deck of cards for only the red ones, without removing the black ones from the deck.

* Turn Filter on with Data tab  Filter, or the shortcut Ctrl+Shift+L.

---

* KEY CONCEPT

* Conditional Formatting Explained

* Conditional Formatting automatically changes a cell's appearance (colour, fill, or icon) based on its value, making patterns and outliers easy to spot at a glance without reading every number individually.

* Found on the Home tab, Conditional Formatting works best combined with Sort and Filter to both find and highlight the data that matters most.

* Quick Tips
* Turn on Filter from Data tab (or Ctrl+Shift+L)
* Sort only after selecting ALL data including headers
* Conditional Formatting is under Home tab — great for spotting patterns
* Clear filters before sorting differently — avoid confusion
* Combine tools: sort to rank, filter to focus, format to highlight

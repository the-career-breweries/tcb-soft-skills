* KEY CONCEPT

* Common Cyber Threats & Strong Passwords Explained

* Common cyber threats to know:

* A STRONG PASSWORD:
* Has at least 12 characters, mixes uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols, and is unique for every different site or account — reusing one password everywhere means one leak can compromise every account at once.

---

* KEY CONCEPT

* Two-Factor Authentication & Online Meeting Etiquette

* Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second layer of protection to an account beyond just a password. It combines something known (the password) with something held (such as a one-time code sent to a phone). Even if a password is stolen, an attacker still cannot log in without also having that second item.

* Quick Tips
* Strong password = 12+ characters, mix upper/lower/numbers/symbols — unique per site
* Never click 'You won a prize' or 'Verify account now' links in email/SMS
* Log out of Gmail/cloud on shared lab PCs — use Win+L when stepping away
* In online classes: mute mic, camera on if required, chat for questions
* Keep software updated — updates fix security holes
* When in doubt about an email — ask faculty before clicking links

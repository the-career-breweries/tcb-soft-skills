# 📱 Bad Listening Habits

* What are some signs that someone is NOT listening to you?
* How does it make you feel?

---

# 🔄 What is Paraphrasing?

Paraphrasing is **summarizing** the core idea of what the previous speaker said **before** you share your own opinion.

### The Toolkit:
* *"So, what you're saying is..."*
* *"If I understand correctly, you feel..."*

---

# 🗣️ Controlled Practice

**Student A:** "Social media is ruining our productivity at work."
<br>
**Student B:** *"So, what you're saying is that apps like Instagram are making us less efficient. I agree, because..."*

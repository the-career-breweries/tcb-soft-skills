# ♟️ Executive Presence
## Handling Difficult Questions with PREP & STAR

---

# 📉 The Fluency Trap

**The Situation:** *"Why should we hire you over other candidates?"*

**The Problem:** When we are nervous, fluency becomes our enemy. We ramble. We over-explain. We lose the interviewer's attention.

<div class="highlight-box">
<em>Structure beats fluency under pressure.</em>
</div>

---

# 🏗️ The PREP Framework

*(Best for Opinion & Persuasive Questions)*

* **P - Point:** Direct Answer (1 sentence)
* **R - Reason:** Why do you believe this? (1 sentence)
* **E - Example:** Concrete evidence (2-3 sentences)
* **P - Point:** Restate the main point (1 sentence)

---

# ⏱️ PREP in Action

**Point:** "You should hire me because of my adaptability."

**Reason:** "In this industry, software changes constantly."

**Example:** "During my last internship, the client changed the scope two weeks before the deadline. I learned the new software over the weekend and delivered on time."

**Point:** "That ability to adapt under pressure is why I will add immediate value to your team."

*(Total time: 35 seconds)*

---

# 📉 Situation & Problem

**The Situation:** *"Tell me about a time you failed," or "What is your greatest weakness?"*

**The Problem:** Without structure, stories drag on. We spend 90% of the time explaining the background context, and only 10% explaining how we actually solved the problem!

---

# ⭐ The STAR Framework

*(Best for Descriptive & Behavioral Questions)*

* **S - Situation:** Set the scene (1 sentence)
* **T - Task:** What was your specific challenge? (1 sentence)
* **A - Action:** What did YOU do to fix it? (Focus 70% of your time here!)
* **R - Result:** What was the positive outcome or lesson learned? (1 sentence)

---

# 🚀 STAR in Action

**Situation:** "Last semester, our group project was falling behind."

**Task:** "We had two days left, and one team member stopped communicating."

**Action:** "Instead of arguing, I organized an emergency Zoom call. I reassigned their tasks among the remaining three of us based on our strengths, and I took over the final editing."

**Result:** "We submitted the project an hour before the deadline and scored an A. I learned how to crisis-manage under tight deadlines."

---

# 🔥 The Hot Seat

**Roles:** Interviewer, Candidate, Evaluator (Timer)

1. Interviewer asks a "Hostile Question".
2. Candidate decides: **PREP** or **STAR**?
3. Candidate has exactly **60 seconds** to answer.
4. Evaluator cuts them off at 60 seconds.

*Rotate until everyone has survived the Hot Seat.*

🔥 The Hot Seat: Question Bank

Interviewer: Choose one of the difficult questions below to ask the Candidate.

Persuasive Questions (Needs PREP)

"We have 50 other qualified applicants. Why should we hire you?"
"Why is your degree relevant to this role? It seems unrelated."

Behavioral Questions (Needs STAR)

"Tell me about a time you completely failed at a task."
"Describe a situation where you had to work with a very difficult team member."
